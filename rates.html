Official exchange rates

The rates at which the Bank of Irad values foreign currencies in som. The United States dollar rate is fixed by the Bank of Irad Act 2008; the rates of other currencies follow from it and from international cross rates.

About the official rates

The official rate applies to conversions at licensed banks and to all official purposes: contracts, customs declarations, taxes, fees and court proceedings.

Licensed banks may set monthly limits on cash operations in dollars under Notice 2026/2. Non-cash transfers are not limited.

The Bank of Irad publishes no other rate.

Rates archive

The electronic archive holds the rates for the current day. Rates for earlier dates are issued on written request by the Library and Reading Room of the head office.
