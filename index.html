Notice to the public

The buying and selling of foreign currency is an activity licensed by the Bank of Irad. The Bank publishes only the official rate.

Prices quoted by unlicensed persons in markets and in the street are not rates of the Bank of Irad, are not recognised for any official purpose and carry no protection. Exchange currency only at licensed banks.

The Bank of Irad

The Bank of Irad is the monetary authority of the Irad Special Economic Zone. It issues the som, holds the United States dollar reserves that back it, licenses and supervises the banks, and operates the Bank of Irad Clearing House.

The som and the United States dollar are the legal tender of the ISEZ.

Statistics

Reserve cover and the som in circulation are published in the Bank's Annual Report. The Statistical Bulletin for 2025 is in preparation.
